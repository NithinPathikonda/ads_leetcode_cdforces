## 🛠️ The 3-Step Protocol to Turn a 🔴 Weak Area into 🟢 Mastered

1. **Step 1: Dissect the Canonical Archetype**:
   - Write down the 10-line skeleton template.
   - Trace the algorithm step-by-step with pen and paper on a small test case.
2. **Step 2: Solve 2 Direct Variations**:
   - Same pattern, different problem wording.
   - Force yourself to reuse the exact same template.
3. **Step 3: Post-Mortem & Timed Challenge**:
   - If you needed hints, reset the problem to Day 0 in [REVISION_AND_TIMELINE.md](REVISION_AND_TIMELINE.md).
   - Once you can code it under 15 minutes from scratch with zero warnings, mark it 🟢 **Mastered**!
