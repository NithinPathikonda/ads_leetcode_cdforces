## 💡 The Contest Post-Mortem Protocol (What to do immediately after a contest):

1. **Dump Your Live Thoughts**:
   - What was your initial intuition during the contest?
   - Why did you pick that approach?
2. **Identify the Exact Failure Point**:
   - **WA (Wrong Answer)**: Did you miss an edge case (empty array, negative values, duplicates, overflow)?
   - **TLE (Time Limit Exceeded)**: Was your Big-$O$ too slow for the constraint ($10^5 \implies O(N^2)$)?
   - **Blank Out**: Did you fail to recognize the pattern?
3. **The Upsolve**:
   - Write the optimal C++ solution and understand *why* it works.
4. **Knowledge Plug**:
   - Update [`SKILLS_AUDIT.md`](../SKILLS_AUDIT.md) and [`ERROR_LOG.md`](../learnings_and_error_logs/ERROR_LOG.md) to make sure you never miss this concept again.
